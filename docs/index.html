Does crypto mood move tomorrow's price, or just follow today's?

Each day we turn social and market sentiment into one score per coin, then test whether it predicts the next one to three days of returns after controlling for momentum and volatility. Everything below comes from out-of-sample tests.

Out-of-sample track record

Walk-forward test: the model retrains every 30 days on past data only and forecasts the days after. "Price only" uses momentum and volatility; "+ sentiment" adds the sentiment features. The Clark-West p-value tests whether adding sentiment improves forecasts; below 0.05 means it does.

Strategy line: go long when the next-day forecast is positive, short when negative. No trading costs, so treat it as a picture of signal quality, not a return you could earn.

How the model works

STEP 1

Collect

Daily prices plus sentiment: share of positive posts, number of active users and posts, and the Fear & Greed index. All aligned to UTC days.

STEP 2

Score

Each input becomes a 90-day z-score, weighted by users and post volume, then averaged into one daily score.

STEP 3

Test

Granger tests in both directions with Newey-West errors, and regressions on 1, 2 and 3-day returns with momentum and volatility controls.

STEP 4

Forecast

Ridge regression in a walk-forward loop, compared against a price-only model and split by volatility, attention and trend.
